# Lead Catcher — 30-Minute Staff Onboarding (DRAFT outline)

**Who:** front-desk staff, plus at least one person who can press Yes (Approver, Supervisor or Desk Owner).
**Where:** a test desk with practice data only. Replies go to the **test outbox**. Nothing reaches a real customer.
**Bring:** STAFF_GUIDE.md (printed or open), each person's sign-in. [PLACEHOLDER: session date, trainer name]

All names, numbers and addresses below are **made up** for practice. They are not real people.

---

## 0:00–0:05 — The idea (5 min)
- AIA prepares. You check the facts. A person with approval rights says Yes.
- AIA never sends on its own. It never promises a time or a price.
- Three taps: **Yes** (approve this exact reply), **Stop** (cancel a Yes), **Kill** (close with no action).
- Show the Queue and the Lead Catcher block. Show **Open**.

## 0:05–0:13 — Practice lead 1: water heater leak (8 min)
Typed in by the trainer (**Type in a request**):
> "Hi, this is Pat Example, (312) 555-0100. Our water heater is leaking all over the garage floor. Can someone come tomorrow and what will it cost? 12 Sample Lane."

Steps:
1. Open the card. Read **What they said**.
2. Check the package. Accept the name and phone. Accept or fix the kind of job (Plumbing).
3. See **Scheduling — needs confirmation** and **Price — needs an authorized estimate**. Talk about who handles each, outside AIA.
4. Set an owner, next step and due time. Press **Prepare again** so the draft uses the owner's name.
5. Confirm the phone contact ("Called back and they answered").
6. Accept the draft reply. Show it promises no time and no price.
7. A Responder tries Yes — it's not there for them. An Approver presses **Yes — approve this exact reply**.
8. Press **Run test send**. Show "Sent by AIA (test outbox)".

**Check:** Can each person say why the reply didn't promise "tomorrow" or a price?

## 0:13–0:20 — Practice lead 2: no heat, missing details (7 min)
Typed in:
> "This is Sam Sample, sam@example.com. Furnace stopped working last night. House is cold. Please get back to me."

Steps:
1. See the **Missing** flag for the service address, and the question about it in the draft. Set an owner and next step. Confirm the email contact. Accept the draft reply.
2. Edit the draft and press **Save my edit (new version)**. Show the version number go up.
3. Approver presses Yes. Then **edit it again**. Show the Yes is cancelled.
4. Approver presses Yes on the new version. Press **Stop**. Show the Yes is gone.
5. Trainer (desk owner) turns the test connection **down**. Approver presses Yes; press **Run test send**. It fails. Nothing was sent.
6. Show **Try again** and **I sent it myself**. Record a manual send: pick "Email", answer "Yes, word for word", tick the box. Show "Sent by a person (manual)".
7. Trainer brings the test connection back **up**.

**Check:** Who can press Yes? What cancels a Yes? When does "I sent it myself" appear?

## 0:20–0:26 — Practice lead 3: roof leak + customer reply (6 min)
Typed in:
> "Roof is leaking into the upstairs bedroom after the storm. Jordan Placeholder, (312) 555-0199."

Steps:
1. The helper suggests Plumbing for "leak" and flags "plumbing/roofing" to check. **Fix** the kind of job to Roofing. It also didn't pick up the name: **Fix** it to Jordan Placeholder.
2. Assign, confirm contact, approve and run the first reply (test outbox).
3. Trainer sends a simulated test text from the same confirmed number (a trainer-only test step, not a real text): "Thanks. Can you come today? How much for a tarp?"
4. Show **Customer replied** on the Queue. Open the card. See the new package and the new **Needs a person** items.
5. Set **Waiting** with a reason ("Owner checking today's schedule") and a follow-up time.

**Check:** Where did the reply go? Why does it need a new Yes?

## 0:26–0:30 — Finish and escalate (4 min)
- Record an outcome on lead 1 ("booked", how we know: "Staff recorded it"). **Mark Completed**.
- Kill a spam test card: type a reason, tick the second box.
- Walk through "When to escalate" in the staff guide. Name who to call: [PLACEHOLDER: escalation contact].
