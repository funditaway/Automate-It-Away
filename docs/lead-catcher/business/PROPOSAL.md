## 1. The problem

When a request sits unanswered, or the reply is slow, the job can go elsewhere.

## 2. What Lead Catcher does

Lead Catcher is an Official AIA Pack. It runs inside your AIA account, on your own desk.

1. **Every request becomes one card.** It lands on your desk's Queue. The customer's words, where it came from, and when it arrived are saved exactly as received. Nobody can edit them.
2. **AI prepares the work.** Beside each request, AIA prepares a work package:
   - a short summary
   - details it found (name, phone, email, service, location, what they want)
   - missing information, written as questions to ask
   - a suggested kind of job (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, restoration, other)
   - a suggested next step
   - an editable draft reply, built only from your approved reply wording
3. **A person checks it.** Your staff press **Accept**, **Fix** or **Reject** on each item. Accepting an item never sends anything.
4. **A person approves the reply.** Someone with approval rights presses **Yes** on the exact reply.
5. **AIA sends only that exact version, once.** If anyone edits the reply after the Yes, the Yes is cancelled. It needs a new Yes.
6. **Replies come back to the same card.** When the customer answers, the reply joins their card, and AIA prepares a fresh package.
7. **You record what happened.** Booked, quoted, referred, no answer, not a fit, lost, spam or other.

Every step is written to the card's history. The history can't be changed.

## 3. How approval works

- **AI prepares. AIA manages the workflow, permissions and records. A person checks the facts and says Yes.**
- AI never sends anything on its own. Desk AIs can't hold a seat and can't press Yes.
- **Yes** approves one exact reply: the words, the recipient and the channel.
- **Stop** cancels a Yes before it runs.
- **Kill** closes a card with no action. It needs a reason and a second tap.
- Any edit after Yes cancels the Yes. An approval also expires after a set time (60 minutes by default).
- AIA never confirms a time, a booking or a price for you. If a customer asks "when?" or "how much?", the card shows separate items marked **Needs a person**. The draft says a person will confirm a time and give an estimate.
- If AIA's send fails, your staff can send the approved words themselves and press **I sent it myself**. AIA records that as a manual action by a named person. It never pretends AIA sent it.
