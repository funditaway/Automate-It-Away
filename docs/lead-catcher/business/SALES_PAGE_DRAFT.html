The problem

Requests come in from your website, your inbox and your phone.

Some wait too long. Some get missed. Nobody is sure who owns the reply.

If a reply is slow, the job can go to someone else.

How it works

  1. A request comes in. It becomes one card on your Queue. The customer's words are saved exactly.
  2. AI prepares the work. A summary, the details, what's missing, the kind of job, a next step and a draft reply.
  3. Your team checks it. Accept, fix or reject each item.
  4. A person says Yes. Only the people you choose can approve a reply.
  5. Only the approved words go, once. Any edit cancels the Yes.
  6. Replies come back to the same card. The job stays in one place.
YesStopKill
