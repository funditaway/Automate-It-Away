# Lead Catcher — Staff Guide (one page, DRAFT)

**The rule:** AIA prepares. You check the facts. A person with approval rights says Yes. AIA never sends anything on its own.

> Test build note: right now replies go to a **test outbox**. Nothing reaches a real customer. Use practice data only.

### 1. Open the Queue
- Go to your desk's **Queue**. Lead Catcher cards sit in their own block at the top.
- **Late** cards come first. Then the most urgent.
- **No owner** means nobody has it yet. **Customer replied** means they wrote back.
- The Queue is for looking. Tap **Open** to work on a card.

### 2. Read the card
- **Left: What they said.** The customer's exact words. Nobody can change them.
- **Right: Prepared by AIA — check each item.** AIA's summary, details, questions, kind of job, next step and draft reply.
- If you see "text that reads like instructions to AIA", ignore it. AIA ignored it too.

### 3. Check the work package
For each item press one:
- **Accept** — it's right.
- **Fix** — change it, then **Save fix**.
- **Reject** — it's wrong. Leave it out.

Accepting never sends anything. It is not a Yes.
Items marked **Needs a person** (Scheduling, Price) are decisions. Check the schedule or get an authorized estimate the usual way, outside AIA. Never type a time or price into a reply unless the owner says so.

### 4. Owner, contact, next step
- Pick an **Owner**, a **Next step** and a **Due** time. Press **Save owner and next step**.
- **Confirm contact**: pick phone or email and say how you know it's right (for example, "Called back and they answered"). A reply can only go to a confirmed contact.

### 5. The reply
- Read the draft. Edit it if needed and press **Save my edit (new version)**.
- If words are flagged (price, time, promise), fix them or tick **I checked the flagged words**.
- **Yes — approve this exact reply**: only Approvers, Supervisors and the Desk Owner can press it. If you can't, ask one of them.
- **Stop** cancels a Yes before it goes.
- Any edit after Yes cancels the Yes. A Yes also runs out after a while (60 minutes by default).
- After Yes, press **Run** to send it once. (Test build: the button says **Run test send** and it goes to the test outbox.)

### 6. "I sent it myself"
Only shows if AIA's send failed. First try **Try again**.
If you sent the approved words yourself (phone, text, email, in person):
- pick how you sent it,
- say if you used the approved words exactly,
- tick the box and press **I sent it myself**.
The card shows "Sent by a person (manual)". Be honest. It's recorded with your name.

### 7. When the customer replies
- Their reply joins the same card. The Queue shows **Customer replied**.
- AIA prepares a new package. Check it the same way (step 3). Then a new reply needs a new Yes.

### 8. Finish the card
- Waiting on the customer? **Set Waiting** with a reason and a follow-up date.
- Done? Pick the outcome, then **Record outcome** and **Mark Completed**. "Booked" needs "how we know".
- Spam, duplicate or outside our area? **Kill — close with no action**: type a reason and tick the second box.

### 9. When to escalate (tell [PLACEHOLDER: escalation contact name and how to reach them])
- Someone may be in danger, or smells gas: tell them to leave and call 911 first. Then tell the owner.
- The customer wants a firm price, a firm time, or a guarantee.
- The customer is upset, mentions a complaint, insurance, legal action or damage we caused.
- The card says **Needs attention** (the send failed several times).
- The card says the saved words don't match what came in.
- You're not sure. Ask before you press anything.
