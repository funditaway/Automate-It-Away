What the desk actually does

Catch the work

Note, photo, form, missed call, or a line someone typed. One card. Nobody retypes it later.

Write the next step

Fit, timing, who should see it, what is missing. The queue says what happens next.

Draft the words

The text, the estimate, the reminder, the handoff note. It sits on the card until a person sends it.

Keep the follow-up

One nudge. Then it stops. Money waits only if you wrote that rule.

What happens this week

1. Open a desk

Your name, desk name, city, a code. House, resale, insurance, or something else in your words.

2. Drop work

Try first. Talk it, type it, or use Drop. Real work becomes a Queue card — not a demo.

3. Add a rule if you need one

Fresh desks start empty. A new rule applies to the next card.

4. Send what you approve

The draft stays on the card. Copy it, text it, email it, or hand it to someone. Helpers cannot Stop.
