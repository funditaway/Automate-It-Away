What happens this week

1. It lands

Call, photo, form, or note. Once. Nobody retypes it.

2. It gets sorted

Fit, timing, and risk. If it can hurt money or a customer, it waits.

3. The desk writes it

Estimate, notice, quote, or reminder. Still sitting here.

4. You say yes or no

Then money, if money is due. Then one follow. Done.
