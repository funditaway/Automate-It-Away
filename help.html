How to run the desk

Name a desk. Drop anything. The queue writes the next step. A person sends the draft — or Stops it — before it goes out.

Something broke?

Talk to AIA. We write a card on the AIA Admin Desk. A person looks. Nothing sends itself.

First day

Open
Desk name + desk code. House, resale, insurance, or something else in your words.
Drop
Talk to the desk, type, or attach a file. The desk asks what’s missing. You say drop it. It becomes a card on the queue.
Rule
Fresh desks start empty. Add a wait only if you want one. New drops honor the current list.
History
Past, now, and next on one page. Advanced finds who worked it, files, Grok recs, and audit. Copy or email the story. Nothing sends from History.
People
Search a world @handle. Invite them to this desk. They Accept on their People tab. Email is not a world name. AIA does not send.
Send
Copy, text, email, or hand the draft off. Stop is the owner cancel. AIA does not send the text or the money.
AI agent
Approved crew seat. Drafts on the card. Off desk tap on decision, ideas, editing, working, and ext. Cannot Stop. Cannot move money. Not a public Bot API.

Owner vs helper

Owner
Connects or drops a pipe. Writes rules. Taps Stop. On Desks: explore, rename, close, change the owner code, export, delete, and grant helper edit. Delete writes a log that stays after the shop is gone.
Helper
Works the queue. Needs you, Open, Send when the rule allows, hand the card, phone file. Cannot connect a pipe. Cannot Stop. Cannot delete a desk or change the owner code. May edit shop details only if the owner turns that on.
We type it in
A helper can still take a note. AIA writes the card. A person still taps Send or Stop.
Kind of work
Owner names the work on open, or later on Create. Fields stay on the card. Drop still takes free text if you skip fields.
Packs
Create an automation. Keep it on this desk, list it in pack search, or set a market ask. Other owners search on Create or /market and add it to their desk. A priced pack is a tag. AIA does not take a card for packs yet. Preview is free. Packs do not send money, bind coverage, or pay out.

Pipes across the internet

Now
Webhook in and out. Anything on the internet can write a card to /api/hook. Copy the hook on Pipes. Zapier and Make can post there today.
Search
Type any site on Pipes. Talk it if your hands are full. Matches filter the wall.
Log in
Opens that vendor’s console. It does not send mail, post, or pay. Draft accounts still paste a key and draft only.
Hold · beta
Calendar, SMS, Square, eBay, Consign store — names exist. Stay hold until keys are set. A draft liking the name does not turn them green.
Coming soon
Mail, social, shop, money, CRM, files, talk. On the wall with Log in. Name any other site — the hook still takes the card.
Down
Some marketplaces are not a launch pipe.

Words on the buttons

Needs you
Waiting on a person.
Yes
Go — post it, text it, or mark it sent. Helpers can do this unless a rule waits on the owner.
Stop
Owner, twice. No — cancel it. Only the owner.
Money wait
Only if the owner wrote that rule. Floor can get the job ready. Owner lets the money move.
Open
The job. Notes, extra fields, ask for more.
Save a file
A photo or PDF on the card. Does not send money.
Calendar file
Drops a file on your phone. Google Calendar write stays off until the key is set.
Talk or type it
You tell us. We write the card on your queue. You still send the draft, or Stop.
Drop something off
Family, friend, helper, or staff asks for something. Or paste a list and the desk writes the card. Rules still run. Nobody sends money from here.
Queue filters
All, handed to me, Talk, Drop, Pipes. Empty filter means switch to All or drop something new.
Pipes
Optional. Search a site, copy the hook, or drop a note. The queue works with just a note.
Copy hook
Puts the inbound URL on the clipboard. Paste it into Zapier, Make, or that site’s webhook. Does not send money.
Log in
Vendor console or draft-account key page. Chat login alone cannot draft. Owner still taps Send and Stop.

Login

Desk name + desk code, or email + password. Set a world @handle on Account so people can find you. Email is not a world name.
