Automate It Away Desk

How to run the desk

Name a desk. Drop anything. The queue writes the next step. A person sends the draft — or Stops it — before it goes out.

First day

Open
Desk name + desk code. House, resale, insurance, or something else in your words.
Drop
A note, photo, form, missed call, neighbor text. It becomes a card on the queue.
Rule
Fresh desks start empty. Add a wait only if you want one. New drops honor the current list.
Send
Copy, text, email, or hand the draft off. Stop is the owner cancel. AIA does not send the text or the money.

Owner vs helper

Owner
Connects or drops a pipe. Writes rules, including advanced wait/stop lines. Taps Stop. Lets money move when a rule says wait.
Helper
Works the queue. Needs you, Open, Send when the rule allows, hand the card, phone file. Cannot connect a pipe. Cannot Stop.
We type it in
A helper can still take a note. AIA writes the card. A person still taps Send or Stop.
Kind of work
Owner names the work on open, or later on Create. Fields stay on the card. Drop still takes free text if you skip fields.

Pipes across the internet

Now
Webhook in and out. Anything on the internet can write a card to /api/hook. That is the unlimited pipe today.
Hold · beta
Calendar, SMS, Square, eBay, Consign store — names exist. Stay hold until keys are set. A draft liking the name does not turn them green.
Coming soon
Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, Gmail, Drive, shop apps, QuickBooks, Slack, missed-call voice. On the wall. Not a Connect button.
Down
Some marketplaces are not a launch pipe.

Words on the buttons

Needs you
Waiting on a person.
Yes
Go — post it, text it, or mark it sent. Helpers can do this unless a rule waits on the owner.
Stop
Owner, twice. No — cancel it. Only the owner.
Money wait
Only if the owner wrote that rule. Floor can get the job ready. Owner lets the money move.
Open
The job. Notes, extra fields, ask for more.
Save a file
A photo or PDF on the card. Does not send money.
Calendar file
Drops a file on your phone. Google Calendar write stays off until the key is set.
Talk or type it
You tell us. We write the card on your queue. You still send the draft, or Stop.
Drop anything
Does not have to match a pack. Your words. Rules on this desk still run.
Queue filters
All, handed to me, Talk, Drop, Pipes. Empty filter means switch to All or drop something new.
Pipes
Optional. Calendar, text, webhook. The queue works with just a note.

Login

Desk name + desk code. Email is not the login. Staff get their own code from the owner.
