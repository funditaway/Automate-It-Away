Words on the buttons

Needs you
This job is waiting on a person.
Send
Yes — post it, text it, or pay it. Floor staff can do this when the job is under $250.
Stop
No — cancel it. Only the owner. Press twice so it is not an accident.
Over $250
Floor can get the job ready. Only the owner can let that payment go.

Login

Shop name + desk code. Email is not the login. Staff get their own code from the owner.
