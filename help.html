Owner vs helper

Owner
Connects or drops a pipe. Writes rules, including advanced wait/stop lines. Taps Stop. Lets money move when a rule says wait.
Helper
Works the queue. Needs you, Open, Yes when the rule allows, hand the card, phone file. Cannot connect a pipe. Cannot Stop.
We type it in
A helper can still take a note. AIA writes the card. A person still taps Yes or Stop.
Custom creation
Owner names the work on open, or later on Create. Fields stay on the card. Chat: “this desk is for …” or “add field …”.

Pipes across the internet

Now
Webhook in and out. Anything on the internet can write a card to /api/hook. That is the unlimited pipe today.
Hold · beta
Calendar, SMS, Square, and named pipes wait until they are actually connected. A name on the wall is not a live pipe.
Coming soon
More named pipes stay on the wall. Not a Connect button.

Words on the buttons

Waiting on a person.
This job is waiting on a person.
Yes
Go — post it, text it, or mark it sent. Helpers can do this unless a rule waits on the owner.
Stop
No — cancel it. Owner, twice.
Money wait
Only if the owner wrote that rule. A helper can get the job ready. Owner lets the money move.
The job.
The card. Notes, extra fields, ask for more.
Save a file
Drops a file on your phone.
We type it in
You tell us. We write the card on your queue. You still tap Yes or Stop.
Pipes
Optional. Calendar, text, webhook. The queue works with just a note.

Login

Desk name + desk code. Email is not the login. Staff get their own code from the owner.
