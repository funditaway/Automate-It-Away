The world AIA Account

One person, one AIA Account. That book is what a world user signs up for. Desk name + desk code, email + password, or continue-with on a connection partner. After a world signup you still name the first desk and pick a desk code. A world handle is how people find you on People. It is not an email and not a login.

  • Owner taps stay on the owner seat: Stop, money, delete a desk, desk code, pipes.
  • Members, family, friends, and helpers never Stop or send money.
  • Insurance desks say Insurance / Quote It Away. They never say Vita on the account, the widget, or a pack face.
  • Each It Away shop keeps its own revenue, legal entity, and licenses. They do not share customer money or regulatory hats.

Special API Access with connection partners

Continue-with is identity only. A connection partner may attach to the AIA Account so you can open the same book. That login is not a pipe, not a draft key, and not Special API Access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar write, payments, policy systems, bind, premium, or commission.

  • Scopes we ask a partner for: sign-in identity only — openid, email, and profile / name. Never Gmail. Never Drive. Never Calendar write. Never contacts. Never payments.
  • What we store from that login: provider name, subject id, and if they give it, a name and an email. Apple Hide My Email and GitHub noreply addresses are not used to merge accounts.
  • How we use it: open or link one AIA Account. Session on this phone. That is the limited use.
  • What we never do with a partner token: Send, Stop, pay, draft, bind, place premium, pull commission, sell a list, score credit, or train a public model on your shop book.
  • Paid pipes and draft keys stay on Connections. Owner connects those. A world login does not mint them.
  • Unlink a door on Your account if another door remains (desk code, password, or another linked login).
  • Export the book. Leave this phone. Logout every phone. Close-account stays HOLD until the live delete path exists.

Partners we list on the wall: Google, GitHub, Apple, Microsoft, X, Amazon, Facebook, LinkedIn, Discord, Vercel, Yahoo, and Ask for Grok / ChatGPT / Claude / another site. A door stays Hold until the app id is on this box. Ask means that vendor has not admitted AIA as a website login yet.
