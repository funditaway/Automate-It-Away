Your AIA Account

Each person has one AIA Account. That is what you sign up for. You can sign in with a desk name and desk code, an email and password, or a "continue with" button from a sign-in partner. After you sign up, you still name your first desk and pick a desk code. Your handle is how people find you on People. It is not an email address and not a way to sign in.

  • Only the owner can Stop, handle money, delete a desk, change the desk code, or set up connections.
  • Members, family, friends, and helpers can never Stop or send money.
  • Insurance desks are named Insurance / Quote It Away. They never say Vita on the account, the widget, or a pack.
  • Each It Away shop keeps its own revenue, legal entity, and licenses. Shops do not share customer money or regulatory duties.

Signing in with a partner (Special API Access)

"Continue with" only proves who you are. A sign-in partner can be linked to your AIA Account so you can open the same account. That sign-in is not a connection, not a drafting key, and not Special API Access to Gmail, Drive, writing to Calendar, payments, policy systems, binding coverage, premiums, or commissions.

  • What we ask a partner for: sign-in identity only (openid, email, and profile / name). Never Gmail. Never Drive. Never writing to Calendar. Never contacts. Never payments.
  • What we keep from that sign-in: the provider name, your account id with them, and, if they share it, your name and email. Apple Hide My Email and GitHub noreply addresses are not used to merge accounts.
  • How we use it: only to open or link one AIA Account and keep you signed in on this phone.
  • What we never do with a partner token: Send, Stop, pay, draft, bind coverage, place premium, take commission, sell a list, score credit, or train a public model on your shop's records.
  • Paid connections and drafting keys are set up on Connections, by the owner. Signing in with a partner never creates them.
  • You can unlink a sign-in on Your account as long as another way to sign in remains (desk code, password, or another linked sign-in).
  • You can export your records, leave this phone, or sign out of every phone. Close-account stays HOLD until the live delete path exists.
