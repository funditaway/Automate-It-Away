When it says Needs you

Grok drafted the work on the desk. You Send or Stop. Staff can Send under $250. Over $250 waits on the owner. Stop is owner only, second tap. A connection only posts after Send, and only if that pipe is live.

5

Leave the rest alone

Qualify, draft, one follow, then it stops. No nag loop. If the desk cannot run that job, it says so and you can send a request. We will not paint a dead pipe green.
