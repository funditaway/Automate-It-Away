How Setup Open your shop Desk

Open a desk for the house or the shop.

Give it a name and a code. That pair opens the queue on any phone. Email is not the login.

Yes or noSchool form, oil change, missed call. The desk writes the next step. You press Send or Stop.
OwnerCan cancel a job. Can let payments over $250 go.
HelperCan say yes on small jobs. Cannot cancel. Cannot move big money.

Do this in order

1

Name the desk

Your name, desk name (the house, the shop, or both), city, owner or helper, what this week looks like, email, desk code.

Open your shop →

2

Sign in the same way on the next phone

Desk name + that person’s code. Do not share the owner code.

Sign in →

3

Drop the first thing in

School form. Oil change. Missed call. Job photo. Type it, drop a photo, or tell the desk.

Queue → · Tell the desk →

4

Say yes or no

The desk already wrote the reminder, estimate, or text. Send means go. Stop means cancel. Payments over $250 wait for the owner.
